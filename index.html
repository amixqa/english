02 / GEOGRAPHY

Location and Geography

Santorini is located in the southern part of the Aegean Sea. It belongs to Greece and is part of the Cyclades island group.

The island lies southeast of mainland Greece. Its landscape includes steep cliffs, small villages, beaches and views of the open sea.

Santorini has a volcanic origin. A large volcanic eruption in ancient times changed the shape of the island and helped create its famous caldera.

  • Country: Greece
  • Sea: Aegean Sea
  • Island group: Cyclades
  • Main town: Fira
  • Landscape: Volcanic cliffs and beaches
  • Climate: Mediterranean

03 / HISTORY

History of Santorini

Santorini has a long and fascinating history. In ancient times, the island was known as Thera. People lived here thousands of years ago.

One of the most important archaeological sites is Akrotiri. It was a settlement of the Bronze Age. Archaeologists have discovered buildings, streets, pottery and wall paintings there.

A major volcanic eruption in the second millennium BC buried much of the settlement under volcanic material. This helped preserve many remains.

Over time, Santorini became known for its villages, maritime history, farming and distinctive landscape. Today, its history attracts visitors and researchers.

Visitors can learn about ancient life by exploring archaeological sites and local museums.

04 / ATTRACTIONS

Famous Places

Oia

Oia is a picturesque village in the north of Santorini. It is famous for white houses, blue domes and beautiful sunsets.

Fira

Fira is the capital and main town of Santorini. Visitors can explore narrow streets, museums, shops and restaurants.

The Caldera

The caldera is a large volcanic basin surrounded by steep cliffs. It offers spectacular views of the sea.

Why visit these places?

They offer beautiful views, traditional architecture, local history and many opportunities for photography.

05 / GEOLOGY

The Volcano and the Caldera

Santorini is part of a volcanic area in the southern Aegean Sea. Its dramatic landscape was formed by volcanic eruptions over a long period.

A caldera is a large depression created when the ground above a volcano collapses after a major eruption. Santorini's caldera is partly filled by the sea.

The volcanic islands of Nea Kameni and Palea Kameni lie inside the caldera. Nea Kameni is known for its dark volcanic landscape and walking paths.

The volcano is an important part of Santorini's identity. It explains the shape of the cliffs, the different colours of the rocks and some of the island's unusual beaches.

This landscape makes Santorini interesting for tourists, geologists and history lovers.

06 / NATURE

Beaches and Nature

Santorini has unusual beaches because of its volcanic history. The sand and stones can be black, red or grey.

Red Beach

Red Beach is known for its reddish cliffs and dark volcanic stones. The colours create a dramatic landscape near the sea.

Perissa Beach

Perissa is famous for its long stretch of black volcanic sand. Visitors come here to swim, relax and enjoy the coastal scenery.

Kamari Beach

Kamari is another well-known beach with dark sand, seaside cafés and a promenade. It is a popular place for a relaxing walk.

The contrast between blue water, dark rocks and white buildings makes the island unique.

07 / LOCAL LIFE

Culture and Traditions

Santorini has a strong connection with Greek culture. Its traditional villages, churches, local celebrations and architecture are important parts of island life.

Whitewashed buildings are common in many villages. Their simple appearance creates a beautiful contrast with the blue sea.

Churches and small chapels can be found throughout the island. Religious festivals and local celebrations bring communities together during the year.

Hospitality is an important part of Greek culture. Visitors can experience it in family-run restaurants, cafés and small shops.

Traditional music, local food and handmade products also help visitors understand the island's cultural identity.

08 / GREEK CUISINE

Traditional Greek Food

Food is an important part of Greek culture. Santorini has local dishes made with vegetables, beans, olive oil, herbs and seafood.

Fava

Fava is a traditional dish made from yellow split peas. It is often served with olive oil and onions.

Tomatokeftedes

These are fried tomato fritters prepared with tomatoes and herbs. They are a well-known local speciality.

Fresh Seafood

Many restaurants serve fish and other seafood. Visitors can enjoy these dishes while looking out over the Aegean Sea.

Local Products

Santorini is known for its cherry tomatoes and distinctive agricultural products. Its dry climate influences local farming.

09 / TRAVEL

Activities for Tourists

There are many ways to enjoy Santorini. Visitors can explore the island's villages, beaches and beautiful natural scenery.

Walking and Sightseeing

Tourists can walk along the streets of Fira and Oia, take photographs and admire traditional Greek architecture.

Boat Trips

Boat trips offer views of the caldera and nearby volcanic islands. Some tours also visit coastal areas and hot springs.

Visiting Museums

Museums and archaeological sites help visitors discover the island's history and ancient civilization.

Relaxing by the Sea

Visitors can spend time at the beaches, enjoy local food or watch the sunset. Santorini offers activities for different interests and travel styles.

10 / TRAVEL TIPS

The Best Time to Visit

Santorini has a Mediterranean climate, with hot, dry summers and relatively mild winters.

Spring

Spring is a good time for walking, sightseeing and enjoying the island before the busiest summer period.

Summer

Summer is popular for beaches, boat trips and outdoor activities. The weather is usually hot and sunny, but popular places can be crowded.

Autumn

Early autumn can offer warm weather and opportunities to explore villages and coastal areas.

Winter

Winter is generally quieter. Some tourist businesses may have shorter opening periods, so visitors should plan ahead.

Travel tip

Visit popular viewpoints early or arrive before sunset to enjoy the view comfortably.
